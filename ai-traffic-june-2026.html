What Changed: May-June Update

📈
Traffic Surge: 2.77M → 3.85M Requests (+39%)
The May-June period saw the largest absolute increase yet. Infrastructure monitoring panels reached scale limits, requiring dashboard adjustments.
🔺
OpenAI Resurgence: 8.8% → 12.7% Combined
After declining through April, GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot both increased in May-June. Total OpenAI share now back above pre-April levels.
⚠️
New Bot Entrants: 6.8% "Other" Category
Previously negligible (<0.1%), new AI crawlers now comprise nearly 7% of traffic. Increased bot diversity suggests broader AI indexing competition.
📊
Infrastructure Impact: Scale Limits Reached
ICTS reports monitoring panels hitting display limits due to volume. Technical debt in analytics infrastructure now visible at current traffic levels.
Maastricht University

Updated Recommendations

  1. Scale Monitoring Infrastructure
    Current panels cannot accommodate projected growth. Upgrade analytics capacity before next reporting cycle.
  2. Implement Rate Limiting
    With 3.85M requests/month, consider CDN-level rate limiting for aggressive crawlers. Bytespider alone generated ~180 GB.
  3. Investigate New Bot Sources
    The 6.8% "Other" category warrants investigation. Identify which new AI services are indexing UM content.
  4. Cost Impact Analysis
    At current growth rates (39% monthly), project bandwidth costs for Q3-Q4 2026. Present to Digital Strategy committee.
